## Art and narrative contract

- Original, detailed, non-realistic high-resolution pixel art.
- Subterranean impact technology must be visually distinct from the Level 2
  forest: black-bronze shell plates, red-brown cavern tissue, amber membranes,
  magenta feeder roots, pale fiber, and cyan ACO instrumentation.
- Santol anatomy is the primary Capsule Husk/environment mutation language;
  jackfruit fiber and banyan roots support the boss and midpoint reveal.
- Capsule 07 reads simultaneously as a cracked seed, relay, and planetary
  harvester. No asset may present it as a conventional spacecraft interior.
- Raster assets contain no baked English or Thai text.

## Prompt-specific direction

### Parallax stack

The opaque far cavern supplies stratified earth and a deep sinkhole. Four
true-alpha layers add distant shell ribs, amber membrane chambers, near armor
buttresses/root columns, and a dark cable/root foreground frame. The composite
keeps an open combat window while clearly separating the biome from the forest.

### Gameplay tile kit

The individual cutouts supply repeatable capsule-plate ground, left cap,
floating platform, clean up-slope, rotatable corner, broken gap, and a
collision-free santol/jackfruit infestation overlay. Right cap, down-slope,
opposite corner, ceiling, and wall orientations may be derived by deterministic
runtime mirroring/rotation after collision and lighting review; bitmap stretching
is forbidden.

### Hazard, extraction, and landmark

The four-frame santol seed piston moves from sealed socket through a cyan-magenta
tell, fibrous launch, and recovery. The root-chamber extraction beacon preserves
a clean player opening and uses drill feet appropriate to the cavern. The
Capsule 07 landmark exposes a segmented seed core and downward harvester roots,
supporting the midpoint story reveal without baked text.

## Processing and validation

The seed piston was normalized with `tools/Normalize-GridSpriteStrip.ps1` using
four frames, `800 × 800` cells, bottom alignment, baseline 740, and alpha
threshold 1. The normalized file is `Format32bppArgb`; all four cell boundary
counts are zero. The four overlay parallax layers and every tile, hazard,
extraction, and landmark candidate contain alpha; the far cavern is the only
intentional opaque RGB layer. Visual QA covered an in-memory five-layer composite
and a source contact sheet. Runtime crop, parallax speed, collision seams,
hazard timing, Web texture memory, landmark placement, and 1280 × 720 combat
readability remain.
