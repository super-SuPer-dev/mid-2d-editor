## Locked pixel-art contract

- Visible square pixels, stepped contours, compact cluster shading, limited
  color ramps, and selective dithering.
- No painterly brushwork, smooth gradients, antialiasing, photorealism, or 3D.
- Salak and nipa anatomy anchors the Thai-local-fruit theme; wetland roots,
  conduit tissue, and drowned rural infrastructure support it.
- Transparent assets retain real alpha and contain no baked language text.

baseline 740, alpha threshold 1. Every internal boundary contains zero visible
pixels. Contact-sheet review confirmed pixel clusters, readable traversal tops,
open combat space, true alpha, and distinct tell/active/recovery frames. Runtime
collision, filtering, placement, memory, and 1280 × 720 validation remain.

history, and must not be used at runtime.
