## Locked pixel-art contract

- Authentic detailed pixel art with visibly square pixels, stepped contours,
  compact cluster shading, limited color ramps, and selective dithering.
- No painterly brushwork, smooth gradients, antialiasing, photorealism, or 3D.
- Longan flesh/seed supplies the sensory-eye language; dragon-fruit bracts form
  the final-biome crown. Neural roots remain secondary tissue.
- Transparent assets retain real alpha and contain no baked English/Thai text.

## Processing and validation

The four-frame platform strip was normalized to four `800 × 800` cells with
bottom alignment, baseline 740, and alpha threshold 1. Every internal boundary
has zero nontransparent pixels. Contact-sheet review confirmed authentic pixel
clusters, readable walkable surfaces, a clear central combat corridor, true
alpha on every cutout, and distinct stable/warning/active/collapse frames. The
opaque far background is intentional. Runtime placement, collision, filtering,
parallax speed, texture memory, and 1280 × 720 projectile readability remain.

The v1 package from commit `75b2f89` was rejected because its rendering was
smooth painted illustration rather than the approved pixel-art direction. It is
retained only in Git history and must not be used at runtime.
