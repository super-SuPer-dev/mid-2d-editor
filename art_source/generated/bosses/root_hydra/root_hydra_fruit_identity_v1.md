# BOSS-ROOT-HYDRA fruit identity v1

**State:** Review (identity and first body-animation batch only)

**Primary fruit:** Nipa-palm fruit cluster / พวงลูกจาก

## Structural mapping

- The round cluster of tightly packed, dark wedge-shaped nipa fruits becomes
  the boss's breakable central armor crown.
- Four separated wedge fruits become armored snapping heads at the ends of
  long, fibrous root-necks, providing four readable projectile origins.
- Copper-orange fruit fibers form the inner jaws and brighten before attacks;
  ragged nipa leaves form neck fins that amplify direction and recoil.
- Pale-aqua nutrient fluid and cyan alien veins connect every head to the
  protected marsh-conduit core beneath the crown.

## Mechanic mapping

The four heads support alternating crossfire, offset radial rings and moving
water-lane walls. Each mouth glow is a pre-fire tell. Breaking heads seals their
stumps with cyan sap and opens the central conduit weak point. Fruit anatomy is
therefore functional combat language rather than decoration.

## Art contract

Detailed high-resolution original pixel art; non-realistic, non-cute and
text-free. Dark umber and wet olive define armor and leaves, copper-orange
defines mouth danger, and cyan defines conduit energy. The silhouette stays low
and rooted even when necks extend. All runtime body strips use four 1000 x 900
cells, bottom-aligned to baseline 840, with true alpha and no interpolation.
