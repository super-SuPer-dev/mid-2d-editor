# Possessed Banyan fruit identity sheet v1

**Canonical boss ID:** `boss_possessed_banyan`

**State:** Review

**Primary fruit:** Jackfruit / ขนุน

**Secondary fruit:** Banyan fig / ลูกไทร

The Level 3 boss is an uprooted banyan consumed by Capsule 07. Jackfruit
remains the dominant combat read; banyan figs act as distributed sensors and
the banyan's aerial/buttress roots provide locomotion and arena control.

## Structural mapping

| Fruit structure | Hostile anatomy | Gameplay function |
|---|---|---|
| Hexagonal jackfruit rind | Large green-yellow chest plates | Armored phase; violet seams widen before the heart becomes vulnerable |
| Golden fibrous jackfruit flesh | Tendons binding the chest and root arm | Brightens and stretches during root sweeps and the phase-break tell |
| Large glossy jackfruit seeds | Vertical seed-column projectiles | Fall in authored columns with telegraphed safe gaps |
| Sticky jackfruit latex | Amber-violet sap glands and ground pools | Creates bounded slowing area-denial zones |
| Small red-purple banyan figs | Sensor nodules on hanging aerial roots | Light in sequence to preview diagonal root-line origins |
| Banyan aerial and buttress roots | Root arm, four heavy legs and arena lines | Drive sweeping melee, trunk charge and temporary lane control |

## Silhouette and value check

- The armored form is tall and top-heavy with one vertical jackfruit chest,
  one long forward root arm and hanging fig-bearing aerial roots.
- The large fruit plate remains readable through its hexagonal edge pattern and
  central mass without relying only on green/yellow hue.
- The exposed phase splits the vertical rind to reveal a narrow bright fibrous
  heart, changing both value grouping and weak-point silhouette.
- Falling seeds are heavy ovals; diagonal roots are long tapered lines; sap is
  a low flat pool. The three arena-control elements remain distinct in value.
