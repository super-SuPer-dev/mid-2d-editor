# Maw Bloom Sovereign fruit identity sheet v1

**Canonical boss ID:** `boss_maw_sovereign`

**State:** Review

**Primary fruit:** Mangosteen / มังคุด

**Secondary fruit:** Durian / ทุเรียน

The Sovereign is a royal mutation of the Level 2 young-mangosteen Maw. Durian
is limited to its crown and armor language so the mangosteen jaw, flesh and
seed remain the primary read.

## Structural mapping

| Fruit structure | Hostile anatomy | Gameplay function |
|---|---|---|
| Thick mangosteen rind | Purple jaw plates and radial bloom petals | Opens during bite anticipation and exposes the core after the phase break |
| White segmented flesh | Padded teeth/iris around the central seed | Frames the weak point and compresses to communicate aiming or stagger |
| Glossy dark seed | Central eye-like sensory core and detached seed bullets | Exposed damage target; turns toward the player before aimed volleys |
| Durian thorn rind | Green-gold crown armor and projectile rear barb | Communicates the protected first phase and sharp projectile direction |
| Green calyx | Crown hinges, pod caps and summon-bud braces | Connects the body, falling pod and ground nest as one biological system |
| Fruit juice and spores | Toxic-lime pores, splash and puddle | Creates falling area-denial projectiles and a bounded ground hazard |

## Silhouette and value check

- Armored form: low, broad six-root stance with a forward jaw and asymmetrical
  high durian crown; it does not share the standard Maw's compact oval outline.
- Exposed form: radial rind petals and a high-contrast white flesh ring isolate
  the central seed even when hue is removed.
- Spore pod: compact rounded falling shape with a calyx cap and dripping tail.
- Seed bullet: narrow horizontal seed-and-barb shape, distinct from the pod.
- Toxic puddle: low flat bounded splash; summon nest: rooted bud/open flower
  with vertical mass. Their silhouettes remain distinct without color.
